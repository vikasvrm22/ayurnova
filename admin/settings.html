Shipping & Checkout

Tax & GST (Phase 8A)

This store is not GST-registered yet by default - leave "GST Registered" off and every order/invoice will show zero tax, regardless of any HSN/rate set on a product variant. Turn it on only once the business is actually GST-registered and you have a real GSTIN to enter.

GST Registered
Off = every order/invoice shows zero tax, no matter what variants are configured. Turn on only once actually registered.

Invoice numbers are allocated from a single, concurrency-safe internal counter and are not guaranteed to be gap-free (a failed/retried checkout can leave a gap) - they are always unique, in order, and never reused.

Payment Gateway

Razorpay Test/Production credentials are managed on the Integrations page (encrypted at rest, never shown in full once saved).

Returns (Phase 6B)

💳 COD Refunds
Allows staff to record manual/offline refunds (UPI/Bank Transfer) for Cash-on-Delivery returns on the Returns page. Off by default - COD refunds are blocked server-side while this is off.

Uploads & Image Compression

Every uploaded product image is automatically converted to WebP and compressed to the target size below - this is what actually controls Storage/bandwidth cost, not just a reject-if-too-big limit.
